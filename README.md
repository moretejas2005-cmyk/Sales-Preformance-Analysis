Sales Performance Analysis
An end-to-end analysis of historical sales data to uncover trends, seasonal patterns and top-performing products, delivered as an interactive Power BI dashboard.
Built by Tejas More during my Business Analytics internship at Codec Technologies.
Dashboard preview

Objective
Analyze 18 months of sales transactions (Jan 2025 – Jun 2026) to answer:
•	How are sales and profit trending month over month?
•	Which products, categories, regions and channels drive the most revenue and profit?
•	Are there seasonal peaks and dips the business should plan for?

Tools
•	Excel – data cleaning, summary tables (KPIs, monthly, product, region, category, channel analysis)
•	Power BI – data model, DAX measures, interactive dashboard with slicers
•	Tableau – optional alternative view (not included)

Dataset
1,500 orders with: Order ID, Order Date, Product, Category, Region, Channel, Quantity, Unit Price, Sales, Profit, Salesperson, Month, Year.
Note: this is a sample/practice dataset, not real company data.
Key Metrics
Metric	Value
Total Sales	₹9.43 Cr (94,299,978)
Total Profit	₹1.46 Cr (14,612,271)
Total Orders	1,500
Total Quantity	4,449
Average Order Value	₹62,867

Key Insights
1.	Electronics dominates – about 73% of total sales, led by Laptop Pro 14 (~₹2.90 Cr, roughly 31% of all sales).
2.	Accessories earn better margins – ~21.7% profit margin versus ~14.8% for Electronics, despite far lower revenue.
3.	Sales are seasonal and uneven – the strongest months were May 2026 (~₹75.4 L) and Oct 2025 (~₹69.8 L); Aug 2025 was among the weakest.
4.	West is the top region (~29% of sales); the other three regions are close together.
5.	Retail Store and Partner channels bring in the most revenue; Online trails behind.

How to Use
1.	Download the .pbix file and open it in Power BI Desktop (free).
2.	Use the slicers to filter by year, region, category and more.
3.	Open the Excel file to see the raw data and the summary sheets behind the dashboard.
